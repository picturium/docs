Use the grouped `crop=` parameter to select a region of the source before picturium
resizes it. Crop dimensions and offsets are source pixels; output `w`, `h`, `ar`, `fit`,
and `pad` are applied afterwards.

```text
/photo.jpg?crop=w:800|h:600|g:center&w=400
```

## The crop parameter

Join `key:value` pairs with `|`. Short and long key names are interchangeable, but the
keys themselves are case-sensitive. Set at least one of `w`, `h`, or `ar`.

<DataTable
  head={['Key', 'Accepted value', 'Default', 'Effect']}
  cols={['k', '', 'd', '']}
  rows={[
    ['w · width', 'unsigned integer', 'unset', 'Width of the crop in source pixels.'],
    ['h · height', 'unsigned integer', 'unset', 'Height of the crop in source pixels.'],
    ['ar · aspect_ratio', 'auto · square · video · fraction', 'unset', 'Aspect ratio of the crop.'],
    ['g · gravity', 'position · attention · entropy', 'image.crop_gravity', 'Places or selects the crop region.'],
    ['x', 'signed integer', '0', 'Moves the region horizontally from its gravity position.'],
    ['y', 'signed integer', '0', 'Moves the region vertically from its gravity position.'],
  ]}
/>

`w:0`, `h:0`, and empty dimension values are treated as unset. A crop containing only
placement keys, such as `crop=g:center|x:20`, is invalid. Unknown keys, malformed
segments, and invalid values also return a client error.

```text
/photo.jpg?crop=width:800|height:600|gravity:top-left
/photo.jpg?crop=ar:16/9
```

## Choosing the region size

The supplied dimensions determine the crop rectangle:

- `w` and `h` select that exact size; `ar` is ignored when both are present.
- One dimension without `ar` spans the source on the other axis.
- One dimension with `ar` derives the missing side from the ratio.
- `ar` alone selects the largest matching rectangle that fits inside the source.

`ar` accepts `auto`, `square`, `video`, or a fraction such as `4/3` or `16/9`. A custom
ratio must be a fraction: `ar:1` is invalid, while `ar:1/1` and `ar:square` are valid.

For a 1000 × 600 source, these definitions select:

<DataTable
  head={['Definition', 'Selected size', 'With center gravity']}
  cols={['k', 'd', 'd']}
  rows={[
    ['w:400', '400 × 600', 'x 300, y 0'],
    ['h:200', '1000 × 200', 'x 0, y 200'],
    ['w:400|ar:2/1', '400 × 200', 'x 300, y 200'],
    ['ar:square', '600 × 600', 'x 200, y 0'],
  ]}
/>

A requested rectangle larger than the source is reduced to the source bounds instead of
failing.

## Gravity and offsets

`g` places the crop rectangle within the source:

<p class="fmt">top · right · bottom · left · top-left · top-right · bottom-left · bottom-right · center · attention · entropy</p>

The default comes from `image.crop_gravity` and is `center` unless configured otherwise.
This is separate from the top-level `g` / `gravity` used by `fit=cover` and
`fit=contain`.

After gravity places the rectangle, positive `x` moves it right and positive `y` moves
it down; negative values move it left or up. The final position is clamped at every
source edge.

```text
/photo.jpg?crop=w:800|h:600|g:top-left
/photo.jpg?crop=w:800|h:600|g:center|x:100|y:-50
/photo.jpg?crop=w:800|h:600|g:bottom-right
```

## Smart crop

Use `g:attention` to favour visually salient detail or `g:entropy` to favour the most
information-rich region. libvips chooses the position, so `x` and `y` have no effect.

```text
/portrait.jpg?crop=ar:square|g:attention&w=600
/texture.jpg?crop=w:800|h:450|g:entropy
```

For an [animation](/docs/animation), picturium applies the grouped crop to every frame. Smart crop is
therefore evaluated per frame; use a fixed gravity when the selected region must not
move between frames.

## Crop, then size

Cropping and `fit=cover` solve different steps. `crop=` chooses the source region first;
`w`, `h`, `ar`, and `fit` then determine the output made from that region.

<TransformFlow />

The later stages are documented in [Filters & effects](/docs/filters) and
[Watermarking](/docs/watermarking).

Explicit rotation changes the source before crop coordinates are calculated. Crop
values are not multiplied by `dpr` or `scale`; they remain pixels of that oriented and
rotated source.

```text
# Select an 800 × 600 source region, then resize it to 400 × 300.
/photo.jpg?crop=w:800|h:600|g:center&w=400&h=300

# Select the largest square region, then make a 600 × 400 cover output from it.
/photo.jpg?crop=ar:square&w=600&h=400&fit=cover&g:top
```

The grouped crop also applies to each [extracted video frame or clip](/docs/animation#video-frames--clips)
and each frame of an animated image. It is skipped when picturium returns the original source or a document
response with [`f=pdf` or `f=svg`](/docs/documents).

For output sizing after the region is selected, see [Sizing & layout](/docs/sizing).
